I am a PhD candidate in Mathematical Sciences at **Politecnico di Torino** (XL cycle), advised by Giacomo Como and Fabio Fagnani. I study how traffic evolves on networks shared by many kinds of users, and how to steer it: through **stability analysis** of multi-commodity dynamical flow networks, **convex optimal control** of freeways (speed limits, ramp metering, dynamic routing), and **tolls** that remain optimal when drivers choose routes noisily.
